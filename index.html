Questions answered

What is Evenos?

Evenos is a mining app where cute robots do the work. You adopt a bot, it mines $EVN in the background, and you collect the rewards.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Mining runs in the Evenos cloud, so a phone or laptop is all you need to manage your bots.

How do I earn $EVN?

Send your bot on missions, join squads and complete daily challenges. Better upgrades mean higher rewards.

Is Evenos safe?

Contracts are audited and you keep control of your own wallet. Never share your seed phrase with anyone.

When does the token launch?

The public launch is planned for Mission 3. Join the waitlist to get early access.
